How to Play

1. Goal: Navigate your player piece from START to FINISH while testing your adjective vocabulary!

2. Draw: Click the UNO card stack to see how many spaces you advance (1 to 5 spaces).

3. Answer Challenges: When you land on an adjective square, answer the multiple-choice prompt (Antonyms, Synonyms, or Context Fill-in-the-blanks) before the timer expires.

4. Scoring: Correct answers grant +10 points. Incorrect answers or time-outs will prompt a retry or no bonus point!

5. Special Tiles: Watch out for Action spaces like Jump ahead, Easy Shortcut, or Cold Freeze!
